V1B Notifications

Privacy policy

Updated 14 October 2026

Purpose and information used

This personal application is intended to send notifications to its owner. When configured, it uses the sender and recipient addresses, notification content and Google OAuth credentials necessary for delivery.

It requests only Gmail's send-email permission. It does not request permission to read, delete or modify existing emails.

Storage and sharing

Credentials and delivery records are kept in private storage with restricted access, not in the public website or public source repository. Notification content is sent through Google to the owner's designated mailbox.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, provided to third parties for unrelated purposes or used to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Credentials are retained while delivery is configured. The owner can stop sending, remove stored credentials and delivery records, and revoke authorization through Google Account connections. Messages already delivered remain subject to the owner's mailbox settings.

These information pages

These pages contain no login form, analytics scripts or application cookies. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical access information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

For questions, use the support contact shown in the application's Google authorization configuration. Do not share passwords or authorization tokens in public.
